Methods and sources

Sources. Estimates come from a third-party property website. Assessment and tax sources are listed in each property record. County-sourced records are labeled individually and show amounts billed separately from recorded payments. Other tax amounts come from the third-party website and do not establish payment. County collection is in progress. The address inventory comes from Santa Cruz County parcel records.

Geography. City-limit flags use the county parcel boundary field, or the original city GIS flag where the county match is unavailable. Mailing-city names do not establish jurisdiction. Outside Santa Cruz city includes other municipalities and unincorporated areas. County inventory coverage is broader than completed tax and estimate collection.

Calculations. Assessment difference = estimated value minus assessed value. Annual tax difference = 1% of estimated value minus reported annual tax. Ratios divide the reported assessment or tax by the estimate. The 1% reference excludes local debt, fixed charges, exemptions and supplemental assessments.

Limitations. Estimates are not appraisals. Tax years and estimate dates can differ; assessment years are often unavailable. Missing values are excluded, not estimated. Address matching, duplicate units and multi-parcel properties can affect comparisons. The tax subset is not a random sample. Differences cannot be attributed entirely to Proposition 13.

Proposition 13. California generally limits annual assessed-value growth to no more than 2%, with reassessment commonly triggered by a change in ownership or new construction. Individual acquisition histories are not established by this dataset.
