The manufacturer's $850 white/gray starter-package page offers Add to cart.
Tax and shipping are excluded. Other retailer search results showed $750–$850,
but stock information was inconsistent or unavailable, so those are not treated
as confirmed buyable alternatives.

**Assessment:** $500 offers a substantial discount from the comparable new
price, but it is not the cheapest used Pronto observed. It sits between the two
verified used listings. The local pickup convenience may justify some premium;
with the handle wear, asking about $400–$450 would be reasonable as a negotiation
starting point, not an estimated market value. Confirm the model, included
canopy/seats, and condition before treating the new price as an exact comparison.
Two used comparables are too few to claim a reliable market median.

Other brands can be much cheaper: the same REL search verified a used stroller
wagon in [Watsonville at $80](https://www.facebook.com/marketplace/item/1396436539222066/)
and [Los Banos at $85](https://www.facebook.com/marketplace/item/1072933895649129/).
Those are broader category alternatives, not evidence of what a Pronto is worth;
both were outside the configured 10-mile radius. Travel/shipping costs are not
included in any comparison.
